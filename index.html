What we do

Built end to end

From the first sketch to the release notes, we handle the whole thing.

Android apps

Native Android, written in the current stack and tested on real hardware. Fast cold starts, small download size, and no surprises on older phones.

Everyday utilities

Tools that do one job and do it instantly. Network helpers, converters, counters, and cameras that open before you have finished reaching for them.

Web apps and sites

Static sites, dashboards, and browser tools that load in a blink and stay up. Nothing heavier than the job actually needs.

Privacy by default

We collect as little as an app can function with. Where data belongs to you, it stays on your device or in your own cloud account, not on a server of ours.

Launch and growth

Store listings, screenshots, release tracks, and the slow honest work of reading the numbers afterwards and fixing what they point at.

Support and updates

An app is not finished at launch. We keep ours current with new Android versions, new policies, and whatever users write to us about.

How we work

Four steps, no theatre

A short loop, repeated until the thing is genuinely good.

STEP 01

Scope it down

We start by cutting. The version worth building is usually the one with three features, not thirty.

STEP 02

Build it small

Working software early, on a real device, in your hands. Opinions get much sharper once something is tappable.

STEP 03

Ship it

Store assets, policy checks, staged rollout. We would rather release a small thing this week than a perfect thing next quarter.

STEP 04

Watch and fix

Crash reports, reviews, retention. Real usage always disagrees with the plan, and it is usually right.

What we care about

Software that respects the person holding the phone

Plenty of apps are built to extract something. We would rather build the one someone actually recommends to a friend, which turns out to be a much simpler brief.

That means no dark patterns, no manufactured urgency, and no permission we cannot justify in one sentence.

  • Works offline wherever the job allows it
  • No account required to use the basics
  • Your data stays on your device or in your own cloud
  • Every permission has a reason we can explain
  • Ads placed at natural breaks, never mid task
  • Every app has a real human to email
