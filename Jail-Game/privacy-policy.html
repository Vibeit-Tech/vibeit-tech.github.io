Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Jail Game app for mobile devices, together with any related services operated by VibeIT Tech (collectively, the "Application"). VibeIT Tech is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider". The Application is a free game supported by advertising and may offer optional in-app purchases.


Information Collection and Use

The Application does not require you to create an account. The Application collects information when you download and use it. This information may include information such as

  • Your device's Internet Protocol address
  • Your device's advertising identifier
  • The screens of the Application that you visit, the time and date of your visit, the time spent on those screens
  • The time spent on the Application
  • Your device model and the mobile operating system you use

Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the Application or clearing its data removes them.


Advertising

The Application is free to play and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, coarse location derived from the IP address, device information, and information about your interaction with ads. The Service Provider does not use this information to identify you personally.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, or anywhere else local law requires it, the Application asks for your consent through Google's certified consent platform before ads are requested. If you decline personalised ads you may still see non-personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time from the privacy options inside the Application. You can also reset your advertising identifier or opt out of ad personalisation in your device's Android settings.


In-App Purchases

The Application may offer optional in-app purchases, for example removing ads or unlocking game content. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. The Service Provider never receives or stores your payment card or billing details. To deliver what you bought, the Application receives only purchase information from Google Play, such as the product purchased, a purchase token or order ID, and the purchase status. Refunds are handled under Google Play's refund policies.

Purchases cost real money. You can require authentication for purchases in the Google Play Store app settings, which is recommended if children use your device.


Cookies and tracking technologies

The Application or its third-party SDKs may use cookies, SDKs, pixels, and similar technologies to support functionality, advertising, analytics, or service delivery. Where required by applicable law, the Service Provider will obtain consent before using non-essential tracking technologies.
